<b>TRIfAma - a modern Tox client for Android</b><br>
<br>
TRIfAma is a free, open-source Android client for Tox, the peer-to-peer
encrypted messenger. It brings a refreshed chat experience to friends,
conferences and groups while keeping the Tox protocol, encrypted local
storage and existing call/file-transfer plumbing.<br><br>

<b>Private by design:</b><br>
Tox encrypts messages and calls end-to-end and does not require a
central chat server. Your message database and received attachments are
stored encrypted on the device.<br><br>

<b>Modern chat UI:</b><br>
* Clean conversation list and chat screens with light/dark themes<br>
* Aligned message bubbles, inline time/status and a rounded composer<br>
* Reply, search, selection, paging and swipe-to-quote preserved<br><br>

<b>Core features:</b><br>
* One-to-one chats, legacy conferences and NGC groups<br>
* Voice and video calls<br>
* File transfers with encrypted local storage<br>
* Optional push wake-ups via UnifiedPush-compatible distributors, ntfy.sh or
  sunup<br>
* Optional persistent group peer list (experimental; see in-app privacy
  warning)<br><br>

<b>Push privacy note:</b><br>
Push wake-ups can save battery and data, but push servers may see timing
metadata. Enable only the push providers you trust.<br><br>

<b>Free and open source:</b><br>
No ads. TRIfAma is free as in freedom and price, released under the
project's GPL open-source license. UI artwork is original project
vector/shape resources.<br><br>
